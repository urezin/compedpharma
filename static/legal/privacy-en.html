This policy explains how CompePharma (compepharma.com) uses personal data. Section 5 and the schedule at the end list what applies specifically to CompePharma.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Dimitrios Gkikas, trading as PQRA (sole proprietorship), VAT no. EL131680720, Leof. Kifisias 44, 151 25 Marousi, Athens, Greece. For any privacy question or request, write to info@compepharma.com or info@compepharma.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, organisation, role, and your sign-in details: a password stored only in hashed form, a sign-in link, or the identifier returned by Google or Microsoft sign-in where the site offers it.
  • Purchase data: what you bought, amounts, invoice details (name, address, VAT number). Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Newsletter data: email address, sign-up date and consent record, and whether emails were delivered.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or contact form.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device, pages requested and error logs, collected by our servers for security and operation.
  • Content you submit: comments, files and records you add to the Site.
  • Advertising data: on sites that show Google ads, Google and its partners collect cookie identifiers and information about your visits, as described in our Cookie Policy.
  • Customer data in our apps: data business customers store in our platforms (see section 6).

We do not ask for special categories of data (such as health data) about you. Please do not send us patient-identifiable information.

We use service providers who process data on our behalf under contract (GDPR art. 28):

  • Render (website and application hosting);

  • Resend (sending account and newsletter emails);
  • Namecheap (domain and email forwarding);

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

See the schedule at the end of this policy for the data CompePharma processes, why, and for how long.

6. Data you store in our platforms

Where a business customer stores personal data in one of our platforms (for example contacts in the CompePharma app, literature-screening records in VigiEye, or validation records in VigiRadar), the customer is the controller and PQRA is its processor. We process that data only on the customer's instructions and under a data processing agreement. If your data is in such a platform, please contact that customer first.

7. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. We transfer data only where the European Commission has found adequate protection (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients) or under the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your account is open, then deleted within 6 months of closure, unless needed for the records below.
  • Invoices and tax records: for as long as Greek tax law requires, normally 5 years from the end of the tax year, longer if the law requires it.
  • Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe; the consent record is kept for 3 years after that, as proof.
  • Messages: up to 2 years after the matter is closed.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.
  • Customer data in our platforms: as set in the customer's agreement.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. You may object at any time to direct marketing.

Send requests to info@compepharma.com. We reply within one month, which may be extended by two months for complex requests. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You may also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, Kifisias 1–3, 115 23 Athens, www.dpa.gr, or to the supervisory authority where you live or work.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls, hashed passwords, backups and limited staff access. No system is fully secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.

11. Children

The Site is for professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

12. Changes

We will post any update here with a new effective date, and tell account holders by email about significant changes.
