Data Processing Agreement

Ελληνικά

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") applies whenever a business customer ("Customer") stores personal data in the CompePharma app at compepharma.com/app (CRM, HR, Recruiting and Document Center). It forms part of the Terms of Use (section P6) and is accepted when the Customer creates an account or buys a module. It meets the requirements of article 28(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Parties and roles

The Customer is the controller. The processor is Dimitrios Gkikas, trading as PQRA (sole proprietorship), VAT no. EL131680720, Leof. Kifisias 44, 151 25 Marousi, Athens, Greece, contact info@compepharma.com ("PQRA").

2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

PQRA hosts and processes Customer Data only to provide the app to the Customer: storing, displaying, exporting and deleting the records the Customer enters, and running the document approval and e-signature workflow. Processing lasts as long as the Customer's account exists, and ends with deletion under section 9.

Data subjectsPersonal data

4. PQRA's obligations

  • Process Customer Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are these Terms, this DPA and the Customer's use of the app, including where data is transferred outside the EEA, unless EU or Greek law requires otherwise (PQRA will then tell the Customer first, unless the law forbids it). PQRA will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.
  • Ensure that anyone authorised to process Customer Data is bound by confidentiality.
  • Apply the security measures in section 6.
  • Help the Customer, as far as reasonably possible, to answer data subjects' requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). The app's export and delete functions are the main tools for this.
  • Help the Customer with security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation (GDPR arts. 32–36), taking into account the information available to PQRA.
  • Make available the information needed to show compliance with article 28, and allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the Customer or an auditor it mandates, on reasonable notice, at most once a year unless a breach or a supervisory authority requires it, and at the Customer's cost.

5. Sub-processors

The Customer gives general authorisation for the following sub-processors:

  • Render Services, Inc.: application hosting and database;
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and its affiliates: payments and billing (account email and billing details only, not Customer Data).

PQRA will tell the Customer by email at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor that processes Customer Data; the Customer may object on reasonable grounds and, if no solution is found, cancel and receive a refund of any prepaid, unused period. PQRA imposes the same data protection obligations on each sub-processor by contract and remains responsible for them. Customer Data is not sent to AI providers.

6. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic; passwords stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes; signed session cookies (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite); every record tied to the Customer's account and checked on each request; sign-in rate limiting; access to production systems limited to PQRA; backups by the hosting provider; prompt application of security updates.

7. Personal data breaches

PQRA will notify the Customer without undue delay, and where feasible within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data, with the information the Customer needs to meet its own obligations.

8. Transfers outside the EEA

Where a sub-processor processes Customer Data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Deletion and return

The Customer can export all Customer Data (JSON or CSV) and delete single records or the whole account at any time from the account menu. Deleted records and accounts are removed from the live database at once. When the Customer's last paid module ends, the account remains available for export for 6 months and is then deleted with all Customer Data, unless EU or Greek law requires PQRA to keep it. Copies in the hosting provider's backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

10. Liability and precedence

Liability under this DPA follows the Terms of Use, except where the GDPR provides otherwise. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on the processing of Customer Data, this DPA prevails. Greek law governs this DPA. Customers who need a signed copy can ask at info@compepharma.com.
