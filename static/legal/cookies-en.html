This policy explains how CompePharma (compepharma.com) uses cookies and similar technologies. It forms part of our Privacy Policy. The operator is PQRA (Dimitrios Gkikas, sole proprietorship), contact info@compepharma.com.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels in emails. We call them all "cookies" here.

2. The types we use

  • Strictly necessary: needed to run the Site, keep you signed in, remember your cookie choice, secure forms and process payments. These do not need consent (Law 3471/2006, art. 4(5)).
  • Preferences: remember settings such as language. We set them only with your consent, unless you choose the setting yourself on the Site.
  • Analytics: count visits and see how pages are used, so we can improve the Site. Set only with your consent.
  • Advertising: sites that show Google AdSense ads (see section 4) let Google and its partners set cookies to serve and measure ads, which can be based on your visits to this and other websites. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent.

3. Your choice

On sites with advertising, visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland first see Google's consent message, with Consent, Do not consent and Manage options buttons. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in that message. You can also opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings or at youronlinechoices.eu. How Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser; blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Site from working.

Local storage. The app keeps one item in your browser's local storage: cpDaily (set by PQRA, preference you choose yourself), a marker saved only when you subscribe to CompePharma Daily from the app, so the sign-up box is not shown again. It contains no email address, is never sent to us and stays until you clear it in your browser.

Fonts. Our web fonts are served from our own server; no font request goes to Google or another provider.

Google sets its cookies under its own policies. Payments take place on Stripe's own checkout pages, under Stripe's privacy policy.

5. Emails

Our newsletters may record whether an email was opened or a link clicked, to measure delivery. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email.

6. Changes

We update this policy when the cookies we use change, and post the new effective date here.
